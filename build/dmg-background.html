Opening it for the first time

Inkboard is free and isn’t notarized by Apple, so macOS asks you to confirm it once.

  1. Open Inkboard from Applications. When macOS says it can’t verify the app, click Done.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll down.
  3. Next to “Inkboard was blocked”, click Open Anyway and confirm.
